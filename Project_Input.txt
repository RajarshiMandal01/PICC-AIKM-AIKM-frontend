Service 1 — manageBucket
Does createBucket also provision the Milvus collection? Another service will be called from createBucket to provision the Milvus collection.  This call will be in asynchronous mode.  The Milvus collection name will be the name as input for BucketName.  This provision service will have a callback method to return success or failure.  Accordingly the status column of nnp_km_buckets will be updated.  I think we should have an additional column to capture errors in case of failure of MILVUS collection creation.
CreateBucket will create a bucket under a single account.
updateBucket will have capability to map a single bucket with multiple account or changing the accounts already mapped with that bucket.
